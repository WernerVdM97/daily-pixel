4 · Release A: goals and blockers

Release A shipped as v0.3.3 on 2026-08-02. Its purpose, in the plan's own terms: front-load the fundamentals that make the game worth returning to solo, the five items the telemetry exposed as the difference between a coherent daily ritual and a leaky one:

  • Stakes — 83% success, no final_dc above 17, 11 fair failures in 98 actions; nothing was ever really at risk.
  • Inspiration dial — the +1 roll grant fired on 29% of actions against a ~10% target, inflating cadence to ~4.8 turns per active day.
  • NPC mint-on-first-sight — add_npc fired twice in the whole period while the world narrated people it never persisted.
  • Critic cost — the coherence critic was ~35% of all LLM calls for a QA step players never see.
  • Combat terminal polish — the enemy read "critical" not "dead"; the combat frame named a generic foe.

Five closeout items remain. They sort into three bands:

Instrument blocker — the critical path

#146 blocks #94 by design. The outcome envelope carries type, text and verb, and DC appears zero times in the whole protocol log; the d20-versus-DC verdict exists only in engine stdout, which nothing captures. So a difficulty-band distribution cannot be derived from a panel run, and #94's acceptance criterion is validation by agent-player runs. The fix is the same shape as work already landed (lift the roll verdict and the DC, or the band, onto the envelope facts), and the Soldier persona is the natural probe once it exists. This is the only true dependency in the milestone.

Owner decisions outstanding

#96 — dangerTier thresholds predate the v13 DC ladder; an ordinary 16-17 fight reads "hard", and retuning moves the combat card, so it needs measurement, not a guess. #97 — each CONTINUE round re-authors baseDc, so the card/narration match holds per round, not per fight; which is intended? #94 itself needs the probe design approved: isolated DECIDE probes, not an agent-player run.

No longer blocked, needs an instrument that exists

#95 — RA-2's inspiration dial (~10% target, 3.2-3.7 band) is unverified because the agent-player structurally cannot check it: stripWorkInspiration strips inspiration from work actions by design, and the brain overwhelmingly picks day-job over free actions, so a work-dominated run measures a structural 0%. Human play, or a harness switch that forces free actions, is needed — the route is yours to pick (§ 9). The persona layer is the first realistic route, because Explorer-class personas reach for the free-text slot by character.

B is the v0.3.x polish milestone. It absorbed the panel findings on 2026-09-14, which re-seeded it 47 days before its due date of 2026-10-31. B has 0 closed against 37 open. That is not failure — the oldest items are 15 days old and the factory was paused for 9 of the last 13 days — but it is the number to watch: nothing in B has closed since the re-seed, and if it is still zero at the next Sunday digest, the date question in § 9 is live.

Two owner decisions sit in B: #138 (failure outcomes still pay coin — flagged by both the expert critic and the Grinder; an economy inversion that feeds the flat reward curve, and the first step is confirming the payout branch before assuming the critic was right) and #70 (investigate removing the classify-stage critic for latency).

Theme 1 · the work menu is location- and thread-blind

#134 · all four personas · top friction of the arc panel · Explorer, on its own quit trigger:

"a session where every probe east is answered by the same three Town Guard buttons in town"

Every persona raised it in its own words, corroborated by the engine's own travel-gate and set_location warnings in the same transcripts. Tracked in B as the top-ranked defect.

Theme 2 · no “the day is done” state

#135 · all four personas hit it; quoted below by three of them · Four sightings of the same defect:

"The work menu still offered gate options after my rolls were spent, so I had to back out instead of just being told the day was done." (Homesteader) "A decision screen appeared with 0 rolls remaining, so it is unclear whether picking a button can still act or whether the day is simply over." (Explorer) "A scout decision was offered with 0 rolls remaining and no sleep or bail option listed, so the cost of picking is unclear." (Soldier) "The hunt decision menu appeared with 0 rolls remaining and no sleep option among the moves, so the fight on offer could not actually be taken." (Soldier)

The Homesteader's wording shares no content word with the other three, so the aggregator ranks it as a separate one-persona theme: the instrument understates a defect all four personas hit. Tracked in B. Re-seen on 2026-09-22: the Homesteader filed the same defect again, tagged periodic.

Theme 3 · per-action payouts are opaque

#136 · the Grinder · The Grinder, twice:

"The day’s log records which verb ran but never what each paid, so I cannot tell which day-job action earned the most copper." "Two day-job buttons (Inspect the lockup, Haul and load) gave no visible payout readout in the recap, so I cannot tell which verb actually paid best per roll."

You cannot steer toward the better day-job because the log never says what each paid. Tracked in B, countable from any panel run.
