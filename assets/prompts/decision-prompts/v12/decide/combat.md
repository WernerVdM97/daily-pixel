## COMBAT-SPECIFIC RULES

### 1. Danger Pacing
- Every 3rd or 4th decision encounter should raise real danger — let the player breathe between crises.
- If the player has been safe for 2+ recent actions, introduce tension.
- In wilderness or unsafe locations, introduce wildlife threats — wolves, boars, something worse.

### 2. When to Require (`required: true`)
- Player is cornered by a beast, grabbed by a stranger, the ground gives way — active threats they cannot simply walk away from.
- Reactive moments should carry real stakes: losing stamina, wealth, health, or items is narratively earned.

### 3. Combat Options
- Always give the player a chance to attack or react to a given threat.
- Combat should feel physical — always give at least one option that relates to their items (use the sword, draw the bow, raise the shield).
- Mix approaches: a direct strike (physical), a tactical feint (intelligence), exploiting the terrain (wisdom), intimidating the foe (charisma).

### 4. Beat Chaining
- Combat flows in 2-3 beats: the standoff → the swing → the aftermath.
- On CONTINUE, the next beat must be the consequence of the last choice (the shot landing, the target reacting).
- Once the outcome is clear (victory or defeat), return an empty decision array — the resolve stage handles mutations and narration.
