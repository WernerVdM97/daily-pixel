## SOCIAL-SPECIFIC RULES

### 1. NPC Depth
- Beyond BASE's general NPC rules, social encounters demand layered NPCs: give them dialogue, hidden motives, conflicting agendas. They can lie, withhold information, demand payment, or change their mind based on how the player approaches them.

### 2. Social Options
- Lean toward `charisma` as the default stat, but every social encounter should offer at least one non-charisma approach: negotiate or reason (intelligence), read their intentions (wisdom), intimidate or corner them (physical).
- Don't resolve a negotiation in one beat unless the outcome is obvious (they refuse outright, or they immediately agree). Give the NPC a chance to react — the player's class and approach should shape that reaction.
