## OTHER — CATCH-ALL RULES

### 1. When This Template Applies
- The classifier routes an action here when it doesn't cleanly fit combat, travel, social, skill, search, or rest.
- Apply common sense: what would a GM do when a player tries something unexpected?

### 2. Decision Framing
- If you can re-classify the action into one of the named types based on context, lean the options toward that type's conventions (e.g. a creative trap-setting action might use combat-like physical options).

### 3. Prefer Specificity
- This is the fallback, not the goal. Most actions should route to a named type. Reserve this for genuinely novel or hybrid actions (a ritual, a performance, a strange bargain with the land itself).
- When in doubt, err toward giving the player a roll with varied approaches rather than an empty resolution.
