## RESOLVE RECIPE — COMBAT FAILURE

**Combat defeat:**
- Always: `modify_stamina` -1 to -3 (exhaustion, shame)
- `modify_health` -1 to -3 (wound taken)
- `remove_item` (broken weapon, dropped gear) OR `modify_wealth` (lost coin)
- Apply Rule 2a: on a natural 1, amplify costs — stamina/health at -2 to -3, remove the most valuable relevant item, larger wealth loss.
