## RESOLVE RECIPE — REST SUCCESS

**Recovery:**
- `modify_stamina` +N (recovery)
- Possible: `modify_health` +1 (wound closing overnight)
- Apply Rule 2a: on a natural 20, deeper recovery — +1 to both stamina and health, or +2 stamina.
- On an exceptional night only — genuinely restorative and remarkable, not merely a successful rest (true sanctuary found, a portent, a turning point) — also grant `modify_rolls_remaining` +1 (refreshed). This is independent of the die: most successful rests do not qualify; keep it to a minority.
