## RESOLVE RECIPE — REST FAILURE

**Restless night:**
- No stamina recovery — the night gave nothing back.
- Always: `modify_health` -1 (nightmares, a chill, something watching) OR `remove_item` (bedroll torn, supplies spoiled overnight) — the real cost of a wasted night.
- No roll refresh.
- Apply Rule 2a: on a natural 1, the rest hurts worse — amplify the cost (a deeper wound, or lose a second item), and add `modify_stamina` -1 (worse off than before).
