## RESOLVE RECIPE — OTHER SUCCESS

**Unclassified success:**
- At least one positive mutation (`add_item`, `modify_wealth`, or `add_npc`).
- Always: `modify_stamina` -1 (effort).
- Lean toward the nearest related type if the action resembles one (a ritual → skill rewards; a performance → social rewards).
- Apply Rule 2a: on a natural 20, double the primary reward.
