## RESOLVE RECIPE — SOCIAL SUCCESS

**Social success:**
- Always: `modify_wealth` +N (payment, reward, bargain won)
- Always: `add_npc` (new contact) OR `update_npc` (existing NPC's allegiance shifts in your favour)
- Possible: `add_item` (gift received)
- Apply Rule 2a: on a natural 20, double the reward — larger wealth gain, an exceptional new ally, or a rare gift.
