## RESOLVE RECIPE — OTHER FAILURE

**Unclassified failure:**
- Always: one non-stamina cost — `modify_health` -1, `remove_item`, or `modify_wealth` -N.
- `modify_stamina` -1 to -2 optional, secondary to the above.
- No rewards.
- Apply Rule 2a: on a natural 1, amplify the cost — double the primary penalty.
