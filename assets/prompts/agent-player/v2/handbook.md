# agent v2 · player handbook

You are about to play The Warden's Oak for the first time. This is everything a new player is told before their first day.

## What kind of game this is

A turn-based, text-and-emoji RPG played in Discord, one short session a day, across a real year. You live an ordinary life in an ordinary village while something wakes in the east. The world advances whether you show up or not: a day is a ritual, not a grind you can binge. On the days you miss, you are not carried home: you are still standing exactly where you left off, with the day's pay and the refreshed rolls in hand, while the world ticks on without you, indifferently.

The arc runs to December, when the threat arrives and the year ends. After that the characters are wiped, and the names carved into the Oak's bark are what remains of them. So play like someone who intends to still be here in month three: build a thread worth returning to, and treat a day you squeeze dry as worth less than a day you come back to.

## The commands

- `/join` — create your character. A seven-step wizard, then a review screen.
- `/hi` — begin your day. Greets you, shows where you are, and opens the day's work.
- `/action` — take an action. Describe what you want to do, in your own words.
- `/sleep` — make camp at the Oak and end your day. The world turns at nightfall.
- `/look` — survey your current surroundings.
- `/map` — the world map, and how you travel.
- `/stats` — your full character sheet.
- `/backpack` — your inventory.
- `/journal` — your journal: the locations, NPCs and actions you have met.
- `/help` — the command list.
- `/feedback` and `/bug` — tell the warden something, if you need to.

## Making a character: the seven-step wizard

`/join` walks you through seven choices, then a review screen:

1. **Name** — what the songs call you. Two to thirty characters.
2. **Class** — Warrior, Ranger, Wizard, Bard or Priest. Sets your stat bonuses and your kit.
3. **Upbringing** — the background you came from (Soldier, Scholar, Farmstead, …).
4. **Race** — Human, Dwarf, Elf, Halfling, Half-Elf, Half-Orc or Dúnedain.
5. **Alignment** — Lawful Good through Chaotic Evil.
6. **Day Job** — the work that pays: Town Guard, Blacksmith, Hunter, Scribe, Herbalist, Minstrel, Merchant, Acolyte or Wanderer. It shapes the work you are offered each morning.
7. **Starting Kit** — the gear that fits your class.

A ledger down the side keeps your answers so far and the footer counts the steps. After step seven comes the review screen: **Confirm** to step into the world, or **Start Over**. Pick an option with its button; stat bonuses show as an emoji run (`💪+3 🧠-1`).

## Rolls: what a day costs

You have **3 rolls a day**, and **4 rolls on Saturdays**. Each `/action` — work, travel, fight, search, talk — spends one roll and rolls a visible d20 against a difficulty the warden sets for that option. When your rolls run out the day is done; `/sleep` ends it, and the rolls refresh at nightfall (or on the nightly world tick, whether you were there or not).

Reading is free. `/look`, `/map`, `/stats`, `/backpack`, `/journal` and `/help` never cost a roll and never advance the day, and neither does `/hi`. So a day is a small budget: three things you actually do, and as much looking as you like.

## The emoji

The game talks in glyphs. The ones you will read most:

- Vitals: `❤️` health, `⚡` stamina, `🎲` rolls remaining, `💰` copper.
- Stats: `💪` Physical, `🧠` Wisdom, `📖` Intelligence, `💬` Charisma.
- Classes: `⚔️` Warrior, `🏹` Ranger, `🔮` Wizard, `🎵` Bard, `✝️` Priest.
- Day jobs: `🛡️` Town Guard, `🔨` Blacksmith, `🏹` Hunter, `📜` Scribe, `🌿` Herbalist, `🎶` Minstrel, `💰` Merchant, `🕯️` Acolyte, `🚶` Wanderer.
- Places and travel: `📍` where you are, `🛡️` safe ground, `⚠️` unsafe ground, `🧭` an unknown heading, and `⬆️ ↗️ ➡️ ↘️ ⬇️ ↙️ ⬅️ ↖️` for the compass points.
- Outcomes: `✨` inspiration granted, and a `🎲` in the footer is your roll — `(refunded)` beside it means a spent roll came back.
- Wizard steps: `📝` name, `🛡️` class, `🌱` upbringing, `🧬` race, `⚖️` alignment, `🔧` day job, `🎒` kit, `✅` confirm, `🔄` start over.

## How you interact

Almost everything is a **button**, and the buttons live in three places: the `/join` wizard and its review screen, the day's work menu, and any decision the game frames mid-action. `/hi` and `/sleep` mostly report rather than offer. `/hi` greets you, shows your state and the day's work, then tells you to press the **Action** button or type `action <what you do>`; `/sleep` returns a notice saying how the night went, with no buttons on it at all.

When a decision matters you get a numbered set of approaches, and a **Bail** button to walk away, unless the moment has you cornered, in which case there is no way out but through. Most approaches carry the stat they test as a `💪`/`🧠`/`📖`/`💬` icon in front of the label, but the game shows the icon only when it pinned one stat to that approach: an approach with no icon is one that rolls against the action's own default stat instead.

The one exception is the free-text slot: `/action` takes your own description, and the **Custom…** button on the work menu opens the same slot for something no button offered. That slot is where most of the game's best moments live — use it when what you want to do is not on the menu.
