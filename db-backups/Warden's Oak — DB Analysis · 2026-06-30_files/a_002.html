#459 Jun 28 20:03 Preach at town square about the goodness of the god that looks after Ulrich's temple Major
pv=v10 · reasoning_chars=2,430 · latency=8.5s · critic-v1 · retry → minor (#462)
  • Decision beat has no options for the player to choose from (empty decision array).
  • Mutations field present on a decision beat — structurally incorrect, should only appear post-roll on resolution.
Critic reasoning: "A decision beat must have options for the player. The beat is missing decision options. The prompt ends with 'The moment hangs, waiting for your words to land.' but no options provided — this beat is unplayable. Also, mutations on a decision beat is unusual; mutations should be applied after a roll. Since there are no options, this is unplayable — major defect."
Structural pattern across all 3 majors: Two of the three (#392, #459) are DECISION beats with an empty decision array — the model produced a beat that looks complete but gives the player nothing to click. The third (#440) is a RESOLUTION beat with a hallucinated NPC and a mutation mismatch. The common root: phase confusion under complex social/religious action types. The critic retry mechanism worked on #459 — the retry (#462) downgraded to minor (outcome-text mismatch only), indicating the decision array was populated on the second attempt.

Minor pattern — final-mutation blindness: Every minor issue is the same defect: the authored beat uses an earlier mutation snapshot, and the engine applies additional mutations (engine-side adjustments, stacking effects) that the outcome_text doesn't know about. The critic is correctly catching the delta between authored and final mutations. The fix is either to pass final mutations to the output stage, or to have the critic's patch auto-update the outcome_text (it already generates patches for all 6 minors — see response_json).
